The Engineering PPM Dilemma

Why Modern Teams Are Drowning Between Fragmented Tools

Project managers live in spreadsheets and legacy PPM suites. Developers and leads live in Jira or Linear. Neither tool understands client contracts, architectural sub-tasks, or profit margins. Workwayz unites both.

Traditional Tools & Issue Trackers

  • Blind to Financials: Ticket trackers have zero concept of Fixed-Bid margins, T&M billable days, LOP deductions, or realized cash.
  • The Estimation Void: Guessing effort without tech stack awareness leads to immediate cost overruns and margin leakage.
  • Architectural Blindspots: Stories are assigned to a single person without breaking down Frontend, Middleware, DB, and Auth impacts.
  • Expensive Cloud Tax: High per-seat pricing models that penalize you as your engineering and contractor team scales.

The Workwayz Operating System

  • Dual Financial Precision: Real-time profit calculations comparing resource cost rates vs client billing rates, milestones, and fixed costs.
  • AI-Powered Tech Estimation: Suggests optimal architectural stacks, horizontal staffing models, and calculates global benchmark effort.
  • Impacted Sub-Tasks Matrix: Deconstruct stories across backend, frontend, and database layers with bulk assignment & grooming readiness.
  • 100% Data Sovereignty: Run on your own cloud or private Docker container with zero per-seat licensing penalties.

Agile purists prefer story points, while clients and finance teams demand billable hours and delivery dates. Workwayz lets project managers configure a conversion ratio in Project Settings or utilize our quick calculator, ensuring developers can estimate in points while contracts are billed accurately.

In Fixed Bid mode, Workwayz tracks contract payment milestones, milestone completion status logs, and compares total resource costs against agreed contract totals. In Time & Material mode, billing is generated monthly based on active billable days, deducting Loss of Pay (LOP) days and adding billable overtime.

When project managers or senior engineers rotate off a project, teams frequently scramble for environment URLs, SSL expiration dates, repository credentials, and backup contacts. Workwayz includes a dedicated BCP register where these items and designated backup resources are tracked in an audit-ready vault.
